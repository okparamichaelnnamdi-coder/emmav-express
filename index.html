Whether you are sending something to a friend or arranging deliveries for your business, our goal is to make the process clear and straightforward.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Our stated base location is Abakaliki, Ebonyi State, Nigeria, with nationwide coverage as the business concept.

Yes. The concept supports individual customers and business partnerships such as restaurants, supermarkets and online vendors.
